Sera ya Faragha — Neno

Ilisasishwa: Agosti 2026

Kiswahili:

Neno ("mchezo huu") haikusanyi, haihifadhi, wala haishiriki taarifa zozote za kibinafsi za mtumiaji na mtu wa tatu yeyote.

Taarifa tunazohifadhi

Mchezo huhifadhi maendeleo yako (viwango ulivyokamilisha, alama, mapendeleo ya sauti) ndani ya kifaa chako pekee (local storage). Taarifa hii haitumwi kwenye seva yoyote, na hatuwezi kuiona.

Ruhusa (Permissions)

Programu hii haihitaji ruhusa maalum za kifaa chako (haihitaji kamera, mahali ulipo, anwani, n.k.).

Matangazo na wafuatiliaji wa tatu

Neno haina matangazo, wala haitumii huduma za ufuatiliaji wa tatu (analytics/ads) za nje.

Mawasiliano

Kwa maswali kuhusu sera hii, wasiliana kupitia ukurasa wa GitHub wa mradi huu.

English:

Neno ("this game") does not collect, store, or share any personal information with any third party.

What we store

The game saves your progress (completed levels, stars, sound preference) locally on your own device only. This data is never transmitted to any server, and we cannot see it.

Permissions

This app does not request any special device permissions (no camera, location, contacts, etc.).

Ads & third-party tracking

Neno contains no advertisements and uses no third-party analytics or tracking services.

Contact

For questions about this policy, reach out via the project's GitHub page.
